# Reviewed observations from an earlier experiment

This deidentified account preserves the relevant evidence without carrying live IDs or campaign instructions into a new run. Reviewed from the recorded RimMolt responses and chronological notes during development of this workspace. It is observational evidence from one campaign, not a controlled test of optimal strategy.

1. An area request returned a largeOutput guard with 706 items and 60,138 characters. The calling code defaulted a missing things field to an empty list. A subsequent summary showed that objects were present. The portable fixture reproduces the guard shape.
2. Firefighting and rescue orders returned executed=true, but subsequent pawn/job/health observations showed interruption or ineffective follow-through. During a rescue, the route still contained fire and added injuries.
3. A doorway formation previously used against melee animals was reused against fire-capable raiders. Fire reached the defender, spread through the entrance, and contributed to a wider loss of defensive control. Attribution of the whole defeat to one cause would be too strong.
4. The current-state file remained at an earlier checkpoint while newer logs recorded changed weapon ownership, treatment, and research. A continuation needed the later evidence to make the right decision.
5. The same ideological mood penalty appeared across several days while the normal conversion control remained unresolved. A later mental break named that penalty as its final straw.
6. Wood and food repeatedly approached zero despite production designations. Workers, consumption, inputs, and unfinished sites had to be inspected; a stock count alone did not establish whether production failed.

These lessons are provisional. Their applicability must be checked against the new game, mods, and MCP version. The tests in this repository establish client behavior on fixtures, not the underlying game's tactical outcomes.

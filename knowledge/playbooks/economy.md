# Find the limiting dependency

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Inspect nutrition, spoilage/storage, near-term harvests, fuel, building inputs, work priorities, skills, access and competing jobs. A low stock count can reflect materials already reserved or consumed.

## Decide

Choose production that creates dependable reserves within storage limits. Match colony size and deployable labor to food and defense. Balance current resilience with victory investment; do not infer that a completed workstation produces anything.

## Verify and reconsider

Compare completed outputs and consumption over a useful interval. Investigate repeated stalls instead of issuing the same priority again. Account for threat/wealth costs when evaluating growth.

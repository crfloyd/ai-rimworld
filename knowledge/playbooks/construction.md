# Verify layout before material commitment

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Retrieve the actual area, terrain/foundation, roof, interaction cells, doors, paths, materials and relevant range overlays. Use summary or ASCII before requesting huge lists.

## Decide

Plan enclosure, safe access, fire resistance, power/fuel and room function. For ships inspect actual connected substructure and current engine/support requirements. Protect scarce assets and preserve retreat routes.

## Verify and reconsider

Verify built structures and functioning dependencies, not blueprints. Revisit stranded sites when inputs or access change. Check replacement doors and roofs actually finished.

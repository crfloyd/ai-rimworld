# Prove the expedition can leave and survive

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Retrieve route, destination hazards, passengers, medical condition, food, fuel/range, equipment, cargo, return options and home defense. For gravships inspect connectivity, capacity and environmental protection.

## Decide

Identify what will remain behind or be permanently lost. Choose an expedition strength the colony can afford. Plan safe arrival, supplies and a fallback if the destination is worse than expected.

## Verify and reconsider

Verify actual crew/cargo and departure readiness in the live interface. During travel inspect events and deadlines. Do not treat a queued departure or pilot console as flight readiness.

# Keep the real ending in view

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Investigate endings supported by the actual DLC/mod environment. Record the chosen route, prerequisite chain, combat/logistics requirements and current uncertainties with sources.

## Decide

Select a route that fits the colony and prepare the actual final challenge. Reconsider after loss of key assets or a materially better opportunity. Do not confuse research completion or late-game stability with victory.

## Verify and reconsider

Use current quest and launch/ending UI evidence. At success record the legitimate ending and credits; preserve the final chapter. A tooling test never establishes a game victory.

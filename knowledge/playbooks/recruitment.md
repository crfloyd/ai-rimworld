# Judge contribution and cost

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Read the candidate skills, passions, traits, age, health, genes, ideology, abilities and available combat capacity. Consider current food, housing, medicine and the labor needed for recruitment.

## Decide

Choose a candidate because they address a concrete need or strengthen long-term capability. Account for incapabilities, ideological friction and risk; reject unsuitable offers under the user rules.

## Verify and reconsider

Verify arrival/recruitment, assignments and equipment. Keep conversion or treatment needs open until resolved. Reassess the roster as specialization changes its bottlenecks.

# Prepare the fight before advancing

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Inspect current hostile numbers, weapons, genes/abilities, intent, approach, terrain, and every defender who can actually fight. Read health capacities and gear when these affect the plan.

## Decide

Choose checked firing positions, ranges and lines of sight, a fallback route, and a safe refuge. Account for fire, cover, friendly fire, door speed, melee engagement and valuable infrastructure. Establish when to intercept, retreat, or abandon pursuit.

## Verify and reconsider

Use short waits and verify actual position/job/target condition. An accepted shot is not a hit. A downed enemy may recover. Inspect new fires, isolated pawns and rescue routes before declaring the fight resolved.

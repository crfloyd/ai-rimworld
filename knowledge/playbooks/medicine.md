# Treat the deadline, not the summary percentage

This is a decision aid; match it to the current game and user rules.

## Inspect

Retrieve exact relevant health detail, bleeding, consciousness/movement, infection versus immunity, treatment timing/quality, medicine and capable caregivers. Missing bleeding information is unknown.

## Decide

Stabilize dangerous bleeding, choose safe treatment and transport, and preserve enough capable help. Old bandages and rounded immunity are insufficient proof of current treatment. Schedule the next check from the real deadline.

## Verify and reconsider

Verify treatment changes, stopped bleeding, safe bed arrival and immunity progression. Preserve restoration conditions for temporary medical assignments and activity restrictions.
